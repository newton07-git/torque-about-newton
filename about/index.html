About me

Innovation, Sports, and the Science of Life.

Howdy! Welcome aboard. If you’re wondering about the name, my dad loved physics and had decided his child would be called Newton long before I came along. It’s been a conversation starter ever since. The extra attention was usually nice, although having teachers expect you to know every science answer had its drawbacks!

The next question is almost always, “Do you know all the laws of motion?” Thanks to my dad, I could recite them by first grade. Growing up, I was always asking what things did, why they worked, and how they were made. That curiosity stayed with me, and by the end of high school, mechanical engineering felt like a natural choice. I enjoy designing products and figuring out how to build them. Seeing an idea turn into something people can use is a big part of why I chose this field.

Outside engineering, sports have always been a big part of my life. I started playing cricket before I started school and went on to play for my school and university, as well as in district and state-level tournaments. Balancing cricket and academics wasn’t always easy, but both mattered to me. I also played soccer for my school and university, and have enjoyed swimming, table tennis, track, volleyball, and basketball along the way. If there’s a game happening, there’s a good chance I’ll want to join in. “Sports freak” is a title I’m happy to keep!

Say hi anytime :)
