About me

Innovation, Sports, and the Science of Life.

Howdy!! Welcome Aboard. The burning question on everyone’s mind seems to be how I ended up with the moniker 'Newton'. Picture this: my father, a man so enamored with physics, had a lightbulb moment long before he was supposed to brainstorm baby names. He proclaimed, 'My child shall be named Newton!' And so, the legend was born. It does come with its perks, like that extra second of attention I snag from new acquaintances—although sometimes, it backfires with teachers giving me that extra attention during my school days. Spoiler alert: I don’t carry apples for demonstrations, but I do engineer some seriously innovative stuff. So stick around, and let's set some ideas in motion!

Swift on the heels of introductions, the inevitable second query always comes hurtling my way: 'Do you know all the laws of motion?' Thanks to a childhood curriculum personally designed by my dad, a first-grade me could recite Newton's laws faster than I could tie my shoelaces. This early dive into the world of science watered a seed of curiosity deep within me. I'm the person who can't just walk past anything intriguing without a barrage of 'What?', 'Why?', and 'How?' questions ricocheting through my mind. This relentless inquiry is the engine behind my passion for innovation, and my drive to build and design new products. By the time the high school graduation cap flew off my head, it was crystal clear that Mechanical Engineering and I were a match made in the meticulously calculated heavens.

Buckle up, because there's more to this Newton than meets the eye! If you're delving into the sports chapter of my life, you'll find it's as action-packed as the science bit. My tryst with the cricket bat began even before my first day at school; I was swinging bats before I picked up pencils! Balancing cricket and academics was like juggling two prized possessions — both demanding but equally cherished. My journey with the willow in hand has seen me sprinting through the ranks from school to university teams, making waves in district showdowns, and even making a mark in state-level tournaments. But why stop at cricket? I've donned soccer cleats for my school and university, sliced through pools, aced serves in table tennis, sprinted tracks, spiked volleyballs, and shot hoops. Yes, it's quite the roster, and I wear the 'sports freak' badge with honor!

Say hi anytime :)
