Project

Evaluated a wind farm concept using site wind data, environmental conditions, turbine comparisons, and a proposed layout.

My contribution

  • Used Weibull statistics and wind rose diagrams to analyze the resource.
  • Compared turbine models and selected a 3.5 MW turbine for the design study.
  • Developed a proposed farm layout with 130 turbines and estimated annual energy production.

Outcome

The work produced a feasibility assessment and a proposed design for renewable energy generation, connecting the site’s wind resource with turbine selection and energy-production estimates.
