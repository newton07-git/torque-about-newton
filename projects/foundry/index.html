Caterpillar · Contract through Midsys Inc. · October 2024–October 2026

Designed foundry components, including chills, cores, and basins, and standardized supplier-part procurement. Supported PPAP and managed MBOMs and engineering releases in Teamcenter.

The work

Designed foundry components and standardized supplier-part procurement, working with quality and manufacturing engineers to support casting operations.

My contribution

  • Designed foundry components, including chills, cores, and basins, for casting operations.
  • Reverse engineered selected components according to the use case.
  • Created models and drawings with specified tolerances to support manufacturability.
  • Helped with the Production Part Approval Process (PPAP) alongside quality and manufacturing engineers.
  • Created and standardized a process flow for supplier-part procurement.

Engineering release & MBOM management

  • Managed the Manufacturing Bill of Materials (MBOM).
  • Maintained engineering design change levels.
  • Uploaded and maintained models and drawings in Teamcenter PLM.

Design to procurement

Created a process flow for supplier-part procurement in collaboration with quality and manufacturing engineers, connecting the models, drawings, and approval work needed to support production.
