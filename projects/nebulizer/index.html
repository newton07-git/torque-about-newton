Portable ultrasonic nebulizer

Product development · Arizona State University · June–October 2024

Led a six-engineer team developing a compact nebulizer from concept and product layouts through working prototypes and manufacturing planning.

6 engineersMultidisciplinary team
Concept to prototypeProduct development scope

Project

Developed a portable ultrasonic nebulizer from the ground up, with an emphasis on translating a product idea into working hardware and planning for scalable manufacturing.

My contribution

  • Led a multidisciplinary team of six engineers across design, prototyping, and manufacturing phases.
  • Led product layout development and working prototypes.
  • Coordinated with outsourcing companies to source essential components.
  • Created and managed the bill of materials and considered health and safety requirements during development.

Why this work matters

Creating a compact nebulizer was a cause I cared about. The goal was to make the device easier to carry and use, with the potential to help more people living with asthma. Bringing a useful product from an idea to working prototypes made this one of the projects I am most proud of.
