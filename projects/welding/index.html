Project

Engineered and assembled a double-axis pneumatic welding machine using rotary and arm-lifting cylinders. The project explored a cost-conscious approach to small-scale welding automation.

Engineering work

  • Integrated rotary and lifting motion into an assembled mechanism.
  • Evaluated extending and retracting forces for the pneumatic cylinders.
  • Worked within available resources to complete the prototype.

Force calculation basis

The ideal cylinder forces below use 40 kPa (0.4 bar), with 40/16 mm rotary and 32/12 mm lift-cylinder bore/rod diameters. These calculated forces exclude friction and pressure losses.
