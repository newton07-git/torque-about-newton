Project

Studied concentrated solar power systems and proposed gold nanoplate-infused Dowtherm A as a heat-transfer fluid.

My contribution

  • Compared the proposed fluid with conventional heat-transfer fluids.
  • Investigated how changes in thermal conductivity could influence heat-transfer performance.

Research focus

The project explored a proposed thermal-management approach to improving CSP performance through heat-transfer fluid properties.
