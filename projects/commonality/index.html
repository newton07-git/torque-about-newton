Mining truck commonality & virtual manufacturing

Caterpillar · Contract through Midsys Inc. · October 2024–October 2026

Built a commonality study from scratch to identify and design reusable truck parts, and led virtual manufacturing activities and cross-system design audits.

From scratchStudy initiated without prior documentation
Part reuseAcross trucks in the same lineup

The design challenge

Find opportunities to reuse and standardize parts across the large mining truck lineup, beginning without prior documentation. The work required looking beyond a single truck to understand where existing parts could serve other machines.

My ownership

  • Initiated and led the commonality study from scratch without existing documentation.
  • Identified parts that could be used on other trucks in the same lineup.
  • Designed parts where there were opportunities for reuse and standardization.
  • Worked with design teams across vehicle systems to develop commonality opportunities.

Virtual manufacturing & design audits

  • Led virtual manufacturing activities for the truck programs.
  • Collaborated with design teams across vehicle systems during design audits.
  • Connected mechanical design work with the manufacturing perspective.

Project focus

The study connected part reuse with targeted design changes to support standardization across machines and identify opportunities for cost reduction.
