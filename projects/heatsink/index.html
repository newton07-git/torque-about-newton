Parallel plate-fin heat sink optimization

Thermal simulation & machine learning · September–November 2023

Combined ANSYS parametric studies with MATLAB prediction models to investigate heat dissipation and pressure drop.

10Simulation setups
100–2000Reynolds number range
R > 0.9ANN correlation

Engineering question

How do fin geometry and flow conditions affect heat dissipation and pressure drop, and can a trained model help predict those responses?

My contribution

  • Built ANSYS numerical simulations to evaluate convection heat-transfer coefficients and pressure drops.
  • Performed a parametric study across ten simulation setups and varied fin geometry and Reynolds number.
  • Developed MATLAB ANN models to predict thermal and flow responses, with reported correlation above 0.9.
  • Explored CNN-based recognition of heat sink geometries using 256 × 256 pixel images.

Outcome

Combined simulation with predictive modeling to explore heat sink configurations. The reported correlation describes the ANN models; it is not a measure of physical product validation.
