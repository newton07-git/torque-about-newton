Engineering question

How closely can the CFD model reproduce reference lift and drag, and how does the flow change as angle of attack increases?

My contribution

  • Simulated the NACA 4412 airfoil in ANSYS Fluent using the Spalart–Allmaras turbulence model.
  • Studied pressure distribution, boundary-layer effects, flow separation, and aerodynamic trends from 0° to 13°.
  • Compared simulated lift and drag coefficients with NASA reference data.

Engineering takeaway
