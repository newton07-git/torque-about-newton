Vacuum bottle thermal reliability

Thermal analysis · Monte Carlo modeling

Investigated material and manufacturing variability with thermal analysis and more than one million Monte Carlo iterations.

40Stainless steel types studied
1M+Monte Carlo iterations

Engineering question

How much can thermal performance change when material properties, wall thickness, and ambient temperature vary?

My contribution

  • Investigated material-property variation across 40 stainless steel types.
  • Used MATLAB and ANSYS for thermal analysis and more than one million Monte Carlo iterations.
  • Modeled wall thickness with a mean of 1 mm and a standard deviation of 0.05 mm.
  • Studied ambient-temperature effects and the sensitivity of heat-transfer performance.

Reported result

In the winter scenario, 12.03% of simulated cases exceeded the 3.5 W heat-transfer limit. The analysis linked insulation performance to material variation, wall thickness, and ambient conditions under the study’s assumptions.
